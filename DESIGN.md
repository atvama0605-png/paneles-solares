## Brand & Style

The design system projects technological authority, ecological permanence, and financial clarity tailored specifically to the high-insolation residential and industrial solar market in Huelva, Spain. The aesthetic pairs the rigorous, engineering-led ethos of photovoltaic technology with Mediterranean warmth and local environmental stewardship. 

The visual style blends modern corporate clarity with tactile, high-conversion micro-surfaces:
- **Tone:** Grounded, certified, transparent, and decisively forward-moving.
- **Visual Strategy:** A luminous canvas with deep emerald anchors, amber-gold solar accents, structured modular grid tiles, razor-sharp information hierarchy, and verified trust indicators (efficiency ratings, subsidies, warranty guarantees).
- **Target Audience:** Homeowners seeking immediate reduction in utility bills, agricultural landowners, and industrial facility managers in Andalusia evaluating high-ticket capital improvements.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to achieve immediate conversion while exceeding WCAG 2.1 AA contrast requirements across all interactive states.

- **Primary Green (`#15803d` / `#166534`):** Represents sustainable engineering, ecological roots, and institutional stability. Used for verified badges, primary navigation links, data anchors, and positive energy savings.
- **Solar Energy Accent (`#d97706` / `#f59e0b`):** Represents photon capture, energy generation, and immediate ROI. Used deliberately for primary conversion paths, high-intent CTA buttons ("Solicitar Estudio Gratuito", "Calcular Ahorro"), flash alerts, and active production metrics.
- **Canvas & Neutral Surfaces (`#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`, `#f1f5f9`):** Pristine, high-reflection surfaces that emulate the clean luminosity of crystalline solar silicon under direct sunlight.
- **Ink & Contrast Slate (`#0f172a`, `#1e293b`, `#334155`):** High-contrast typography colors ensuring effortless legibility in outdoor and bright ambient environments.
- **Functional States:** Success (`#16a34a`), Alert/Pending (`#eab308`), Destructive/System Error (`#dc2626`).

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized across all typographic scales to balance technical geometry with welcoming humanistic contours. The tight letter tracking in larger scale headings provides density, authority, and modern digital punch.

- Headings command attention through bold weights (`700` and `800`), emphasizing figures such as percentage savings, kWp installations, and Andalusian regional grant programs (Subvenciones NextGen / Junta de Andalucía).
- Numerical figures in technical telemetry and financial return cards should enforce tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain vertical alignment in calculators and quotes.
- Monospace styling is avoided in favor of geometric, high-clarity numeral presentation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a flexible 12-column structural grid system anchored by a maximum container width of 1280px to preserve maximum legibility and scan speed.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, and 32px safe margins. Complex conversion modules (e.g., roof solar calculator, quotation form with live bill uploads) utilize 7:5 or 8:4 column splits.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, and 24px canvas margins. Technical cards collapse from 4 columns to 2x2 matrices.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, and 16px canvas margins. Critical lead capture mechanisms and sticky consultation buttons stay pinned to the bottom screen safe area.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections are partitioned with alternating background surfaces (`#ffffff` and `#f8fafc`) with rhythmic vertical padding of `4rem` (mobile) to `6rem` (desktop) to give high breathing room to technical diagrams.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through luminous layering, soft natural shadows, and sharp boundary micro-strokes instead of heavy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base page canvas (`#f8fafc`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** Clean pure white (`#ffffff`) surfaces bordered by a precise, 1px semi-transparent outline (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`). Ideal for standard technical specifications and certification logs.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active States):** Subtly elevated with an ambient, diffused shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Floating & Flyouts):** Used for sticky consultation trigger docks, modal calculators, and customer proof popups: `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.10), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Solar High-Focus (CTA Highlights):** Primary conversion buttons utilize a soft, colored solar glow on interaction: `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a refined modern standard (`roundedness: 2`). This choice delivers 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radiuses on small interactive components, expanding up to 16px (`1rem`) on content containers, and 24px (`1.5rem`) on hero summary cards and estimation dashboards. 

This balance avoids overly sharp industrial rigidity while maintaining the high-grade technical precision expected from an engineering and installation firm. Full pills (`rounded-full`) are reserved exclusively for trust badges, verified certificates, and live status pills.

## Components

### Buttons & Conversion Triggers
- **Primary Action (Lead Generation):** Background in amber solar gradient/solid (`#d97706` hover `#b45309`), text `#ffffff`, font-weight 600, height 48px, horizontal padding `1.5rem`, border-radius 8px. Subtle solar glow on hover.
- **Secondary Action (Technical Verification / Phone):** Background `#f1f5f9`, text `#0f172a`, border 1px solid `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`. Hover background `#e2e8f0`.
- **Tertiary Action (Documentation & Terms):** Ghost format with `#15803d` text and underline transitions.

### Cards & Technical Modules
- Constructed on `#ffffff` backgrounds with an 8px or 16px radius, bounded by a 1px border (`#e2e8f0`).
- Interactive selection cards (e.g., choosing "Vivienda Unifamiliar", "Comunidad de Vecinos", "Nave Industrial") provide a 2px active outline in `#15803d` accompanied by a light emerald tint (`#f0fdf4`) when selected.

### Badges & Trust Anchors
- Compact pill-shaped containers with 4px vertical, 12px horizontal padding.
- **Eco / Certification Badge:** Light green surface (`#dcfce7`), emerald text (`#166534`), accompanied by clean vector checkmarks or solar icons.
- **Local Grant / Subsidy Badge:** Light amber surface (`#fef3c7`), warm text (`#92400e`).

### Form Inputs & Quotation Selectors
- Height 48px, background `#ffffff`, border 1.5px solid `#cbd5e1`, text color `#0f172a`, placeholder text `#94a3b8`.
- Focus state activates an outline with `#15803d` and a subtle 3px ring (`rgba(21, 128, 61, 0.15)`).

### Solar Savings Calculator Widget
- Dual-thumb range slider for monthly electrical bill estimation (`€50` to `€600+`).
- High-contrast readout container utilizing `#0f172a` background with dynamic savings figures displayed in radiant yellow/amber (`#f59e0b`).
